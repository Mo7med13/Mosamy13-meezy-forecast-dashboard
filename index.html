Cash flow forecast. Forecast cash-in follows the selected revenue scenario and the collection-rate assumption. Forecast cash-out is modeled from the supplied P&L using the latest six completed months (Feb–Jul 2026); Aug-2026 is treated as month-to-date and excluded from the baseline. Salary/social-cost run-rate and other cash operating costs are separated from marketplace-vendor payouts. You can override forecast and actual cash-in/out month by month.
